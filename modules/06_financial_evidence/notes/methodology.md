# Methodology note

The financial module is complementary to the project's main mine-level climate analysis.

Financial variables are affected by accounting standards, discount rates, copper prices,
ownership structures, acquisitions, and major investment programmes. For this reason,
the financial evidence is interpreted descriptively and within mine/operator context
rather than as a standalone causal estimate.

Closure provisions are evaluated primarily through within-mine trajectories. CAPEX and
cost variables are examined around documented stress and adaptation episodes, with
particular attention to unit consistency and overlapping investment programmes.

The underlying annual reports used during construction and validation are not included
in the public repository.
