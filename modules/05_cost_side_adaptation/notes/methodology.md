# Methodology note

The cost-side exercise is designed to identify candidate mine-years for documentary
review, not to estimate a causal treatment effect.

Mine-years with unusually weak operating margins or unusually strong capital expenditure
relative to simple mine-specific benchmarks are flagged for review. Operator annual
reports and financial statements are then used to classify the episode as direct
water-source adaptation, precursor/build-phase investment, a water-stress outcome, or
non-adaptation activity.

This manual classification is essential because desalination projects are frequently
bundled with concentrator expansions and because commodity-price or revenue movements can
also generate extreme residuals.

The resulting evidence complements, but does not replace, the project's panel and
event-study analyses.
