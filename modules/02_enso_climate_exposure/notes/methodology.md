# Methodology note

The module separates general climate stress from ENSO-aligned stress.

A shock is treated as ENSO-aligned only when the relevant ENSO phase is active, the
regional teleconnection points in the expected direction, a local anomaly is realised,
and the operational threshold is crossed.

The flood and heat analyses operate primarily at the mine-month level. Persistent
drought is summarised over a multi-year exposure window and related to annual mine
production.

Inference is deliberately conservative because the number of regional clusters is small
and the strongest persistent-drought episode is concentrated in the recent megadrought.
