# Methodology note

Water-scarcity decrees are treated as an administrative response to water stress,
not as a direct physical measure of drought.

The analysis aligns decree activity with DGA water years and July-centered ENSO
conditions and compares the decree series with the physical drought-stress
channel from the project's master stress ledger.

The short post-2008 decree sample and the persistent Chilean megadrought limit
statistical power. Results are therefore interpreted descriptively rather than
causally.
